# The Tit/les Gate

## Point of entry

“Remember the Titans” was not received as an elevation of Greek cosmology. It opened **Tit/les**: the title divided, exposing the letters housed in and working through titles.

The initial tests are preserved as gate conditions rather than converted into explanatory doctrine:

1. Is the mark a **P**, or **?**
2. Is it **ƿyn**, or a **question mark**?
3. Enter by the wordplay of the **Tit/les**, then present the burial goods.

These tests establish a method of attention:

- a familiar shape may carry another letter, name, sound, history, or operation;
- uncertainty is not merely ignorance but may be part of the figure;
- titles are not labels applied after the work; they are active sites in which letters gather relations;
- the “burial goods” are not a bag of symbols but the preserved material relations required for re-entry.

## Burial goods, operationally

For every acquired page, preserve:

- exact title and URL;
- page text or a rights-respecting page record;
- image identity, alt text, caption, source attribution, and placement;
- outgoing and incoming links;
- previous/next relation;
- category and tags;
- rune, letter, sound, and glyph where explicit;
- position in rune order and reflected pair;
- chapter/theme assignment;
- stream or mode (stanza, translation, divination, instruction, alphabet, dialogue, code, riddle, etc.);
- site-authored claims separately from our observations and inferences;
- unresolved readings and transcription uncertainties.

## What the pair does

Peorþ and Sigel are reflected partners, but the chapter does not present them as a stable list of antonyms. Peorþ gathers bodies in a hall around play, laughter, uncertainty, and a textual hole. Sigel places bodies in transit at sea, dependent on an external light that is both hoped for and unstable. The chapter page itself joins those situations: one gathers to play after navigating home; one navigates home in order to gather.

The path repeatedly changes the valuation of its terms. A game can be peaceful and deadly serious. The sun can guide and menace. Battle can be play, pain, historiographic resistance, or the work of thinking with books. These reversals are page-specific actions, not a codebook of symbols.

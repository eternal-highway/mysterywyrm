## Method consequence

No page should be forced into only one reflected-pair cell. The pair matrix is a reliable axis; these other relations are equally valid evidence when explicitly warranted. Page capture must therefore retain:

- archive position;
- chapter/category membership;
- tags;
- previous and next links;
- incoming references when recoverable;
- local lexical, visual, and rhythmic correspondences;
- uncertainty and missing material.

This is how the corpus keeps the structure without confusing it for the whole transmission.

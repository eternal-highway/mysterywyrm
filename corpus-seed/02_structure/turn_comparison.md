## Current warrant

The defensible invariant is modest: chapter reading is carried by links that cross compositional streams while reflected runes exert pressure on one another. Path length, stream order, and semantic center remain variable. This is a corpus observation, not a site-authored theory and not a general law. The later Trust comparison in `chapter_braid_comparison.md` proposes a more specific paired-chapter envelope while retaining this warning.

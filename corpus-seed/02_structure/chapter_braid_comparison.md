## Archive reversal and composition order

The first nine completed paired braids yield 36 independent acquisition-order retrochecks. The four *By Land and By Sea* comparisons are derived from the listing that supplied that path. Axis Mundi adds four prospective direct-walk tests; *They’ll Cut You* and *Moody Joy* add four direct-before-archive comparisons each. *Everything is Temporary* adds three passes and one qualified Translation comparison because *Translating Ear* remains absent from the live tag archive. Its category archive separately matches the full direct path in exact reverse order. The complete Middle path also matches the Twist category archive in exact reverse order. All remain channels from Tonkin's site rather than source-independent corroboration. `braid_archive_order_audit.csv` records the paired-stream acquisition classes.

WordPress archives ordinarily display newest material first while previous/next traversal follows the opposite temporal direction. Combined with *L is for Letters for Titles*, which describes forward composition and backward linking, publication sequence is the leading explanation for the varying partner orders. It remains a strong generative hypothesis rather than an established chronology until exact post dates are captured. The question is relocated: not “which stream causes the reversal?” but “why were the paired pages composed in that sequence?”

## Circuit, chain, and negative result

The closed *Trust* / *Sitting to Battle* seams establish one 34-page circuit:

`ᛇ … Stanza 17: Tiw → ᛋ … Stanza 14: The Game → ᛇ`

The next pages do not form a second reciprocal pair:

`Stanza 19: War Horse → ᛄ … Stanza 12: Year → ᛇ`

Thus *War and Peace* enters *The Future*, but *The Future* enters *Trust*. The second-circuit hypothesis is rejected. *Axis Mundi* enters *By Land and By Sea*, which enters *Light*, which enters *You Have Nothing Else*, which enters *Prosperity*; *Prosperity* enters *The Water Cycle*, *The Water Cycle* enters *Fate*, and *Fate* enters *War and Peace*. The better-supported description is an asymmetric chain feeding a local circuit:

`Everything is Temporary → Moody Joy → They'll Cut You → Axis Mundi → By Land and By Sea → Light → You Have Nothing Else → Prosperity → The Water Cycle → Fate → War and Peace → The Future → Trust ↔ Sitting to Battle`

The reciprocal links are facts of the navigation graph. Their temporal interpretation is not settled. If archive reversal reflects publication order, `Stanza 17: Tiw → ᛋ` may be the forward continuation while `Stanza 14: The Game → ᛇ` is a retrospective backward link. Exact post dates are required before replacing “local circuit” with “chain plus one backward link”; both descriptions remain explicitly distinguished meanwhile.

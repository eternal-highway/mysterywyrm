## What the pair does

Eoh and Tiw are reflected partners, but *Trust* does not assign one to stillness and the other to motion. The yew moves, only too slowly for ordinary human perception; Tiw remains dependable while continually journeying. Both are forms of fidelity in motion.

The chapter distributes trust across different supports:

- roots, branches, bodies, and people who hold one another up;
- signs, stars, roads, covenants, and speech that orient action;
- law, vow, office, and delegated power;
- divine presences that the site refuses to dissolve into safer substitutes;
- evidence that must be interpreted without concealing the point where conjecture begins.

The hostile pages are not exceptions to the chapter. Poison, armament, battle, legal injury, religious displacement, and betrayal disclose what trust costs and what its failure can do. The chapter does not offer trust as credulity or comfort detached from danger.

## The tree / truth pressure

The site repeatedly places the Old English tree-word field beside trust, truth, fidelity, and making with wood. This is a site-authored lexical and compositional pressure, not permission to declare every tree a generic symbol of truth. Eoh remains a particular yew: ancient, useful, toxic, sheltering, grave-associated, and materially rooted.

## Pair pressure

Prosperity is not a generic opposition between abundance and joy. Ing is a divine giver who departs; Wyn is a condition of secured abundance inside walls. Their relation passes through inherited goods, work after patronage, fortified privilege, exile, ship-burial, public reputation, mixed sorrow and joy, divine procession, dangerous transformation, grammar, lineage, and communal memory.

The chapter therefore turns abundance into circulation and boundary. Goods are given, packed, buried, carried, inherited, lost, or left behind. Walls shelter and exclude. The benefactor departs; the banished voice persists. Joy is real, but neither innocent nor self-grounding.

## Burial goods and the ƿyn gate

*Ing is for Scylding* is the corpus's clearest literal presentation of burial goods: body, ship, weapons, armor, ornaments, treasure, companions, lineage, words, sea, and divine destination remain distinct material relations. The page does not merely illustrate the project's earlier phrase; it demonstrates why burial goods are a method of return.

*W is for Ƿ* then places the P/ƿ test inside the category braid itself. P, ƿyn, W, and ? cannot be flattened. The apparent P is a historical letter, a sound, a rune relation, and a performed misrecognition.

## Theological and historical pressure

Ing is received as deity rather than reduced to fertility metaphor. *Ing is for Nerþus* composes Ing, Yngvi-Freyr, Ingun, Freya, Njörðr/Nerþus, procession, peace, fertility, sexuality, sacred grove, bath, and attendant death across a very long historical interval. These identifications remain site-authored historical-theological claims pending source-by-source checking.

Scyld goes to God; the mirror discloses the face God sees; the Rune Poem is presented as a means of addressing gods. These are distinct theological acts within the chapter and should remain distinct before any modern translation.

## Crossing relations

The incoming seam *Stanza 23: Home → ᛝ* extends the chapter topology from *You Have Nothing Else* into *Prosperity*. The Ing glyph's derivation from Gifu and *Translating Wyn*'s relation to fortified home create additional pressure across the Gifu/Eþel and Wyn/Ing pair boundary without replacing either pair.

Ing also participates in the Mann → Lagu → Ing → Eþel tempo chain. *No Z* continues into the exile material of *You Have Nothing Else*, where Z's lament becomes explicit. Navigation direction, narrative continuation, reflection, tempo, and graphic construction must remain separate relations.

*Bliss* and *All in All* remain opaque code. Their placement intensifies the chapter's joy/sorrow and wall/gift fields but does not warrant a solution.

## Envelope result

`paired glyphs → paired castings → mixed compositional interior → paired translations → paired stanzas`

Its partner order is Ing → Wyn in glyphs and castings, Wyn → Ing in translations, then Ing → Wyn in stanzas. This strengthens the recurrent envelope while again falsifying any fixed partner order or universal interior sequence.

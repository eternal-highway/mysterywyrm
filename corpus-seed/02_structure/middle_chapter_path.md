- The Middle is complete as a category path but is not a fifteenth reflected-pair braid.

- Tonkin's Eolhx/helix, lunar-calendar, historical, philological, and astronomical claims remain site-authored until independently checked.
- Completion of the path does not reduce the Middle's significance to its topology.

## Pair pressure

The chapter does not identify peace with passivity or war with motion in a simple binary. Eh gives speed, height, communication, prestige, and violent capacity; Is arrests that power through weather, scarcity, slippery ground, and stillness. Yet ice also becomes beautiful, navigational, lethal, and linguistically productive, while the horse becomes divinatory, communal, vulnerable, and a bearer of speech.

The title therefore names an oscillation among mobilization and restraint, gathering and exile, speech and listening, armed capacity and the material conditions that can make its exercise impossible. Peace is not sanitized: *Friþ* is shadowed by the living and dead; Y's peace follows banishment; winter peace is enforced by hunger and ice.

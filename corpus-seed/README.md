# Letters for Titles — Corpus Seed

Version: 0.26.1  
Established: 2026-08-22  
Primary site: https://lettersfortitles.com/

This is the first durable working corpus for *Letters for Titles*, Vern Tonkin's Alphabet Book.

It begins where the work says to begin: in **The Middle**. It does not assume that chronological publication order, alphabetical order, or the Old English rune sequence is the sole governing order. Each is recorded separately so that their crossings remain visible.

## Governing posture

- Receive this as a **transmission**, not as doctrine.
- Do not reduce the work to a lesson in symbolism.
- Do not treat structural discovery as an exhaustive statement of the work's significance.
- Preserve source, image, caption, title, link, sequence, pairing, category, tag, quotation, code, and uncertainty as distinct evidence.
- Keep observation, site-authored claim, historical claim, and working inference visibly separate.
- Prefer the site's own paths and relations before imposing an external taxonomy.

## Directory map

- `00_orientation/` — the gate, the Tit/les, and entry through The Middle.
- `01_inventory/` — verified pages, visible archive sequence, and resolved stream registers.
- `02_structure/` — the 29-rune turn, matched pairs, chapter themes, topology, page paths, and relations that cross the pair matrix.
- `03_series/` — the distinct compositional streams that cross within each chapter.
- `04_registers/` — correspondences and claims/evidence with explicit warrant.
- `05_method/` — acquisition rules, lineage, and re-entry.
- `06_sources/` — primary and secondary source notes.
- `07_capture/` — page-level records and media manifests.
- `08_assets/` — project-level supplied artifacts kept separate from site evidence.

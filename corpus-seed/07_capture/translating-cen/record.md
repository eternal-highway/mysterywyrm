## Observed

The page treats *Cen* as rare, relates it to Cynewulf's runic signatures and to *cene*, and insists on multiplicity, punning, and saying much with few words. It contrasts *blac* as deathly pallor elsewhere with glittering brightness here, then locates safe indoor torchlight specifically where elites have space to rest.

## Site-authored claims

- Cen can mean torch while its glyph can abbreviate *cene*, bold or keen.
- Old English multiplicity and punning permit several meanings to operate together.
- The stanza's indoor torchlight belongs to elite spaciousness and material fire safety.

## Working inference

Torchlight is knowledge and courage, but also privilege: the living may know the fire while only some can rest securely beside it.

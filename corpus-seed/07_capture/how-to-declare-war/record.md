## Observed

The page makes declaration precede war and tests the authority, voice, scale, medium, audience, and duration of that declaration. State conflict contracts toward conflict in a room, around a table, between two people, and finally a lifelong series of hostilities whose declaration becomes the war itself.

## Working inference

Speech is not preparatory ornament: authorization, address, and relation can constitute the conflict they claim merely to announce.

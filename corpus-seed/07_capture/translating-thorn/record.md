## Observed

It makes bramble pain bodily and sudden, then compares Thorn with the giant of the Norwegian and Icelandic rune poems. `Anfeng` is held between grasping, receiving, defense, and defender; the thorn strikes before the person can choose a clean relation to it.

## Working inference

Thorn's harm is distributed through hierarchy and environment: thegn labor and the leader's protected place make the same bramble unequally dangerous.

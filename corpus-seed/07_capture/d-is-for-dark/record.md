## Observed

The page sends the reader backward into a moonless night where human vision fails while wolves, rats, and other presences remain active. Darkness is not emptiness: it redistributes advantage and makes the sleeping person vulnerable.

## Site-authored claims

- Moonless darkness is presented as a human limit rather than absence of other life.
- The page links darkness to infestation, predation, and unequal perception.

## Working inference

Dæg's universal daylight is counterpressured by a night in which the human is neither alone nor perceptually sovereign.

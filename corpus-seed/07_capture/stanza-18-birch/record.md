## Observed

Beorc is bloomless yet bears, carries twigs for divination without offspring, appears beautiful on its boughs, and rises leaf-laden toward the sky.

## Site-authored claims

- The site's translation preserves bearing without bloom or offspring.

## Working inference

The stanza reverses the expected order of fruition: lack does not prevent bearing, and future knowledge emerges from a tree whose reproduction is denied or suspended.

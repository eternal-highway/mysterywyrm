## Observed

The page instructs the reader to fold the Rune Poem and look at Gift and Home together. Exile removes place, people, recognition, dignity, worth, and support; wilderness is social as much as geographic. Gift and home are therefore paired under the threat of their loss.

## Site-authored claims

- The author identifies Gift and Home as a deliberate reflected pair.
- The chapter names exile as the unthinkable condition that reveals their shared necessity.

## Working inference

“You have nothing else” is not nihilism. It names what remains when ordinary possession collapses: mercy, sustenance, people, memory, justice, and the possibility of return.

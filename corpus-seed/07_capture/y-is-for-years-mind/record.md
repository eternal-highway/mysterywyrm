## Observed

The page begins from `geárgemynd`, the anniversary remembrance of a death, but refuses to confine grief to that day. It moves through memory, inability to touch the dead, the instant of death or delayed news, foreknowledge, and several changing definitions of a year, returning repeatedly to now.

## Site-authored claims

- A year's mind commemorates the dead but does not contain their presence in memory.
- Solar, sidereal, and anomalistic years differ and change.

## Working inference

The chapter's recurrence is wounded. The year returns, but neither calculation nor anniversary converts death into closure.

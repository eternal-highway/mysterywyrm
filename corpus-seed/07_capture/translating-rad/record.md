## Observed

The commentary distinguishes Rad as ride and sometimes road, reads mile-paths through Roman roads and milestones, and contrasts comfortable indoor talk with long bodily travel on a hard horse. Rinca and recyde bring person, hall, governance, and possible official business into the journey.

## Site-authored claims

- Rad means horseback riding or the ride and may mean road in compounds.
- The stanza's rider may be traveling on official business along long Roman mile-paths.

## Working inference

The translation makes authority and infrastructure part of motion while subjecting speech, status, and governance to bodily distance.

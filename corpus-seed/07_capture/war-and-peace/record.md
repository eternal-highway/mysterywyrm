## Observed

The chapter page pairs the armored war horse with ice. A horse powerful in battle can be stopped by frozen roads, accumulated ice, broken footing, and the food burden of winter campaigning. Warfare is therefore seasonal, and ice enforces a period of peace. The page also raises an explicitly uncertain relation between slippery ice and Odin's horse Sleipnir.

## Site-authored claims

- Winter ice and provisioning materially limit mounted warfare.
- The Is/Eh pair may carry a late and uncertain echo of Sleipnir, the slippery one.

## Working inference

Peace is not an abstract moral opposite of war. It can be imposed by weather, hunger, animal vulnerability, transport limits, and the material cost of projection.

# Notes

The 0.23.0 transcription uses only the twenty-eight visible thorn-stave coordinates plus the direct red `Y` and question mark. The seven physical rows join as `HOW UN / COMFO / RTABL / E DO Y / OU WA / NT TO / BE?`, yielding `HOW UNCOMFORTABLE DO YOU WANT TO BE?`. Keep this page distinct from the stanza and translation pages that supply its title phrase; shared language is relation, not solution.

## Observed

The casting addresses gloom and receding warmth, then redirects attention toward an interior fire and returning light. Shadows are presented as light interrupted by the body rather than evidence that light is absent.

## Site-authored claims

- The casting promises the return of light without denying present gloom.
- Shadows are described as light resting upon the body.

## Working inference

Cen locates guidance in a fire that persists through darkness, but the body remains the place where illumination also becomes shadow.

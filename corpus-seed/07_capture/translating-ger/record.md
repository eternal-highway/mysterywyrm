## Observed

The page treats the Ger stanza as a problem of time and ambiguous `bleda`: fruit, blossom, or green shoot. It asks whether the operative season is harvest or spring, turns to solar reckoning, and retains the holy king of heaven as giver of earthly abundance.

## Site-authored claims

- Ger names year as seasonal recurrence rather than one unambiguous date.
- Old English poetic multiplicity keeps fruit, blossom, and shoot active together.

## Working inference

Translation does not select one future. It preserves several seasonal fulfillments while locating their gift in divine agency.

## Observed

The page reads *drihtnes* as God-as-lord and *metod* as fate, measure, destiny, and death. It connects Day to the God stanza through hope and fortune, distinguishes fortunate from wretched people, and makes sunlight both divine message and messenger while retaining translation loss.

Universal access to daylight is not universal social security; the translation lets divine address cross unequal human conditions.

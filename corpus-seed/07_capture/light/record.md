## Observed

The chapter page pairs the Day and Torch stanzas as similar as night and day. It invokes light and dark as interdependent opposites, then interrupts preference for light by asking who actually receives security at night.

## Site-authored claims

- Day and Torch are paired through difference as well as resemblance.
- Light and dark are presented as mutually dependent, not independent absolutes.
- Security through light is unequally distributed.

## Working inference

The chapter refuses both a simple binary and a universal claim that illumination benefits everyone in the same way.

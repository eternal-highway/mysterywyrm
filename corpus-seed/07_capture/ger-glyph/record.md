## Observed

Ger is presented as a small rune whose form, height, sound, and name overlap with other runes. Its carving begins from Thorn and makes the form look both ways through Janus, god of beginnings and doorways.

## Site-authored claims

- Ger shifted through J, palatal G, and Y sounds and sometimes resembles Ior.
- Its name is homonymous with Yr, intensifying an identity problem already present in its changing form.

## Working inference

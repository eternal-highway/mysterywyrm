The files are not byte-identical. After coalescing and resizing the live frames to 360 × 360, corresponding frame RMSE values normalize between `0.01148` and `0.01167`. The large question mark, interlaced wyrm, lower knot, threefold form, animation states, frame count, and timing coincide. AS001 is therefore a resized/optimized derivative of the live site-authored slot-32 animation.

This supersedes the earlier “relation undetermined” classification. AS001's local bytes remain user-supplied; its relation to the site source is mechanically established.

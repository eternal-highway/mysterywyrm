## You Knew it Beforehand — complete rebus, bounded order

The entire carrier is now mechanically transcribed. Its upper field reads `WHAT DO YOU KNOW?`; its lower displayed bands, from top to bottom, read:

```text
AFTER
NO
LIFE
DEATH
```

No arrow, numbering, continuous line, color route, or other carrier mark directs the lower fields into the non-displayed order `NO → LIFE → AFTER → DEATH`. `NO LIFE AFTER DEATH` is nevertheless the uniquely natural full-sentence reading and agrees with the mortality caption. It is therefore retained as a **high-confidence semantic resolution of the rebus**, not relabeled as a mechanical transcription.

The earlier `THERE IS NO LIFE AFTER DEATH` remains rejected: `THERE IS` requires six units absent from the carrier. No further untranscribed face units remain.

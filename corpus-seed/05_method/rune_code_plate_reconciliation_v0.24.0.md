# Rune Code Plate Reconciliation — 0.24.0

Date: 2026-08-29  
Scope: five complete full-resolution plate transcriptions and one bounded partial; no structural acquisition and no continuous-message inference

## Method

Each lead carrier was inspected at the live page's full available source resolution and read only under the site-provided *Octave* group/place table. A physical pair or stave supplies `group.place`; the carrier changes by page. Titles, captions, chapter positions, and the earlier independent plaintext candidates were used only after the counts as confirmation. The primary images remain source-linked metadata rather than retained corpus media.

## Result and limits

The mechanically complete *Octave*-keyed plate set rises from five to ten: *Arrows*, *The Way*, *Present*, *Shh*, *For Anybody Who Rests With Them*, *Battle*, *Always*, *It Never Deceives*, *Bright Fruits*, and *Friþ*. *You Knew it Beforehand* adds one verified upper inscription but is not counted as a complete plate.

These results do not establish publication, archive, folded, or chapter order as one continuous Rune Code text. They show a stable coordinate language expressed through page-local material grammars.

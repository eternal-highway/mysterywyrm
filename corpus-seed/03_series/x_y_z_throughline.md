## What persists

- X, Y, and Z are distinct voices, not algebraic placeholders with stable meanings.
- Three repeatedly collapses toward two: expulsion, subtraction, banishment, mirroring, and mutually defining opposition recur.
- Z is neither disposable remainder nor neutral narrator. Z sometimes mediates, sometimes witnesses, sometimes laments, and sometimes restores the third term that prevents X/Y opposition from becoming a closed binary.
- Games, divination, letters, equations, mirrors, roads, code, death, and domestic space are recurring dramatic instruments rather than detachable topics.
- The strand repeatedly tests whether relation survives separation: memory after death, persons after exile, creator and creature across a screen, observer and observed across a surface, and three bodies that cannot be reduced to a pair.

## The order is deliberately non-identical

Publication order is not a sufficient dramatic chronology. The clearest local proof is *No Z* and *Z’s Lament*. The archive/publication relation places the lament earlier, but the lament speaks as the consequence of the expulsion narrated in *No Z*. The corpus should therefore preserve at least three orders separately:

1. live archive order;
2. reverse-archive / publication direction;
3. dramatic or referential continuations between pages.

The series does not establish a hidden master cipher merely because it is the one major stream outside the two decoded cipher mechanisms. Any cipher test must specify a mechanically recoverable carrier and order before interpretation.
